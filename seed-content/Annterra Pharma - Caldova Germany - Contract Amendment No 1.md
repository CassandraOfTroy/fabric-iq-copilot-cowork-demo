# Contract Amendment No. 1

**Supply agreement CTR-ANN-DE: finished packs, oral solid dose**

**Between:** Caldova Germany GmbH ("Caldova") and Annterra Pharma ("Annterra", supplier code CM-001)

**Signed:** 18 September 2026  |  **Effective:** 5 October 2026

## 1. Purpose

Caldova needs guaranteed production capacity for Allerveo packs from October 2026. Annterra reserves priority production slots for Caldova Germany in return for a higher unit price.

## 2. Changes to the agreement

| Item | Until 4 October 2026 | From 5 October 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Unit price per finished pack (oral solid dose) | USD 3.95 | USD 4.45 |
| Allerveo production slots | Standard scheduling | Priority slots reserved every week |
| Order lead time | 6 weeks | 3 weeks |

## 3. Invoicing and payment

Annterra invoices every working day at the amended unit price for all packs delivered on or after 5 October 2026. Payment terms stay Net 30. If an invoice is more than 15 days overdue, Annterra may release the reserved priority slots.

## 4. Contract administration

Caldova registers the amended unit price in its contract database before the effective date, so that Annterra invoices are matched against the amended price.

## 5. Other terms

All other terms of the supply agreement stay unchanged. The agreement ends on 31 December 2027.
